Ceremony protocol

Know the little things before the big day.

These simple guidelines help the ceremony remain organised, respectful and comfortable for everyone.

🎓

Dress code

Wear neat, formal or smart ethnic clothing beneath your ceremonial robe. Keep footwear comfortable and appropriate for the stage.

🪑

Seating order

Graduates should follow department or roll-number seating instructions. Guests must sit in the section assigned on their passes.

📷

Photography rules

Use personal cameras only where permitted. Keep aisles clear and avoid blocking the view of other attendees.

🔔

During the ceremony

Keep phones on silent, listen for your name and follow instructions from volunteers and event coordinators.

🪪

Identity verification

Carry your student ID and any registration confirmation requested by the convocation team.

♻️

Robe return

Return the robe and accessories to the correct counter after the ceremony. Keep the collection slip until the process is complete.

Need a quick answer?

Frequently asked questions.

Useful answers to common student questions.

Can I register if I am not sure about attending?

Use the attendance option that best reflects your current decision. Check with the college office if you need to update it later.

How many guest passes can I request?

A student can request a maximum of two family guest passes. Final allocation depends on the college's approval and venue capacity.

Can I change my robe size after selecting it?

This demonstration does not save selections. In a real system, contact the convocation desk before the announced robe-size change deadline.

Is the robe security deposit refundable?

The sample fee display is labelled as a refundable deposit for demonstration purposes. The actual amount, conditions and refund timeline must be confirmed by the college.

Does this website make an actual registration?

No. This is a frontend-only semester project. It demonstrates page structure, forms, input controls, validation, responsive layout and CSS interactions. It does not connect to a server or database.

What should I do if my name or degree details are incorrect?

Do not submit incorrect academic details. Contact the relevant department or examination office to confirm the official record.
